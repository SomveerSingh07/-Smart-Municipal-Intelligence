# Municipal Citizen Charter & SLA Policy 2026

## 1. Roads and Pavements
- Pothole and road repair on major arterial roads: SLA 24 hours.
- Residential street repairs: SLA 48 hours.
- Dangerous road cave-in / sinkhole: Barricade in 1 hour, repair in 12 hours. Escalation to Executive Engineer (Roads).

## 2. Water Supply & PHE
- Major pipeline burst: SLA 4 hours (Critical).
- Drinking water contamination report: Immediate shutoff within 2 hours, repair & lab test within 6 hours.
- Low water pressure / irregular supply: SLA 24 hours. Escalation to Superintending Engineer (PHE).

## 3. Drainage and Sewerage
- Open manholes: Immediate safety barricading in 30 minutes, replacement in 4 hours.
- Sewage overflow in residential area: SLA 6 hours.
- Routine storm drain cleaning: SLA 48 hours. Escalation to Health & Drainage In-charge.

## 4. Solid Waste Management
- Garbage collection point overflow / dead animal disposal: SLA 6 hours.
- Daily missed door-to-door collection: Resolution by 4:00 PM same day. Escalation to Chief Sanitation Inspector.

## 5. Electrical & Streetlights
- Sparking poles or exposed wires: SLA 30 minutes (Critical Safety).
- Main intersection dark spots: SLA 12 hours.
- Individual residential street bulb: SLA 24 hours. Escalation to City Electrical Superintendent.
